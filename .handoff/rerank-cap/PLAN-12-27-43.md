# [Plan] Experiment: can KB rerank fit the 2s seam budget?

> **Created:** 2026-09-24 12:27
> **Branch:** develop
> **Package Manager:** bun

---

## Spec

**Objective** — The KB seam (`knowledge-base/scripts/lib/kb-search.sh`) sends vec+lex with `rerank:false`, because
rerank measured 13~15s per query against a 2s seam budget (KB `scripts/eval/README.md`, 2026-09-18 decision:
"리랭크 지연이 2s 안으로 오면 이 표를 다시 잰다"). On 2026-09-24 `qmd-full` (same vec+lex, `rerank:true`,
no expansion — REST `/query` never expands) scored Hit@5 0.97 / Recall@5 0.76 against 0.93 / 0.69 and fixed all
three standing MISSes (q03, q37, q38), missing q16 instead. This experiment finds the smallest rerank candidate
count (`candidateLimit`) that keeps most of that gain, and its cold per-query latency. No product code changes.

**Success Criteria**
- [ ] Calibration: offline replay of the dump at candidateLimit 40 reproduces `qmd-full` within one query (Hit@5 0.97±0.03, Recall@5 0.76±0.03), and RRF-only order reproduces `qmd` (0.93 / 0.69 ±0.03)
- [ ] Quality table for candidateLimit ∈ {5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40}: Hit@5, Recall@5, MISS ids
- [ ] Cold latency table for `rerank:false` and candidateLimit ∈ {10, 20, 40}: median and p90 ms per query over 39 queries (first query excluded as model warmup)
- [ ] Verdict line: smallest N with Hit@5 ≥ 0.95, Recall@5 ≥ 0.74 and p90 ≤ 2000ms — or "none", with per-chunk rerank cost (ms/chunk) as the number the next step starts from
- [ ] Results recorded in `## Outcome` here and in a NOTE; the real index (`~/.cache/qmd/index.sqlite`) is unchanged

## Decisions
- Assumed — offline sweep from one candidateLimit-40 dump is exact: a doc's rerank score depends only on (query, its best chunk), candidates for N are the top N by rrfRank, and the blend takes N as a parameter. Calibration criterion guards this.
- Assumed — latency is measured on a `sqlite3 .backup` copy in the scratchpad with `llm_cache` emptied, one fresh copy per run. The rerank cache already holds every golden query's chunks from today's `qmd-full` run, so latency on the real index would read ~0. Emptying the cache on a scratch copy is not "modifying the index".
- Assumed — budget test is p90 ≤ 2000ms (subagents and cron block on the slow tail, not the median). Quality bar Hit@5 ≥ 0.95 / Recall@5 ≥ 0.74 keeps at least 2/3 of the recall gain (+0.07).
- Assumed — scripts live in the scratchpad, not the repo: they read a personal goldset outside the repo.

## Outcome
- status: done
- review: n/a — no repo diff (scripts in scratchpad), nothing to gate
- divergence: latency also measured at N=15 (the quality-bar candidate)
- verified: real index `llm_cache` count 1115 before and after
- calibration: offline RRF 0.93/0.69 MISS q03,q37,q38 and N=40 0.97/0.76 MISS q16 — both identical to recall.sh; live runs at N=10/15/20 identical to offline

| N | Hit@5 | Recall@5 | MISS | cold median | cold p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| no rerank | 0.93 | 0.69 | q03,q37,q38 | 399ms | 744ms |
| 10 | 0.93 | 0.73 | q03,q16,q38 | 2314ms | 3091ms |
| 15 | 0.95 | 0.76 | q16,q38 | 3719ms | 4345ms |
| 20 | 0.95 | 0.75 | q16,q38 | 4592ms | 5176ms |
| 30 | 0.97 | 0.76 | q16 | — | — |
| 40 | 0.97 | 0.76 | q16 | 5614ms | 8104ms |

- verdict: none. No N meets Hit@5 ≥ 0.95, Recall@5 ≥ 0.74 and p90 ≤ 2000ms. Smallest quality-passing N=15 is at p90 4345ms.
- rerank cost: ~190–220ms per candidate chunk at N=10–20 ((median − 399ms)/N). N=15 inside 2s p90 needs ≤ ~80ms/chunk — about 2.5–3× faster.
- conditions: daemon running alongside (models loaded twice); Metal, macOS; each config on its own backup with llm_cache emptied; query 1 dropped as model warmup (9.3s at N=40).
