# A rerank input cap of 128 tokens keeps the KB rerank gain but stops at ~110ms per chunk, just over the 2s budget

## Context
NOTE-12-58-00 found that no rerank candidate count fits the 2s KB seam budget: about 200ms per chunk against a
needed ~80. `LlamaCpp.rerank` sends each candidate's best chunk whole, up to the context budget. This session
added an opt-in `QMD_RERANK_MAX_DOC_TOKENS` (commit 60a9f8b on `feat/rerank-max-doc-tokens`, not pushed) and
measured caps at N=15.

## Findings
- The N=15 best chunks sent to the reranker (600 = 40 queries × 15) measure 812 tokens median, 1276 p90 and
  1494 max. 99% are over 256 tokens.
- Each run is cold on a fresh `.backup` copy with `llm_cache` emptied, with the daemon up, on Metal, and with
  query 1 dropped. ms/chunk = (median − 399ms no-rerank median) / N.

| cap | N | Hit@5 | Recall@5 | MISS | median | p90 | ms/chunk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| full | 15 | 0.95 | 0.76 | q16,q38 | 4353ms | 4720ms | 264 |
| 512 | 15 | 0.97 | 0.77 | q38 | 3133ms | 3488ms | 182 |
| 256 | 15 | 0.97 | 0.76 | q38 | 2469ms | 2702ms | 138 |
| 128 | 15 | 0.97 | 0.77 | q38 | 2049ms | 2192ms | 110 |
| 64 | 15 | 0.93 | 0.71 | q03,q21,q38 | 2034ms | 2304ms | 109 |
| 128 | 10 | 0.95 | 0.76 | q03,q38 | 1911ms | 2097ms | 151 |

- A cap does not hurt quality down to 128 tokens. It fixes q16, which uncapped rerank loses at every N ≥ 10.
  At 64 tokens quality drops below the no-rerank baseline.
- Latency is not proportional to doc tokens. Going from 128 to 64 saves nothing, so a per-chunk floor of about
  110ms remains. The likely cause is the fixed prompt: template plus query, about 350 tokens per the
  `RERANK_TEMPLATE_OVERHEAD` comment. Unverified.
- Parallelism matters. `RERANK_TARGET_DOCS_PER_CONTEXT` is 10, so N=10 runs on 1 ranking context and N=15 on 2.
  At cap 128, per-chunk cost is 151ms at N=10 against 110ms at N=15.
- Run-to-run noise is noticeable: the uncapped N=15 control measured 4353ms median today against 3719ms in the
  earlier session.

## Decisions
- Verdict: none under the strict bar (Hit@5 ≥ 0.95, Recall@5 ≥ 0.74, p90 ≤ 2000ms). The closest config is cap
  128 at N=15 (p90 2192ms) and cap 128 at N=10 (p90 2097ms).
- Slice 03 (keyword-centred window) is not run. Its condition was "latency passes, quality fails", and the result
  is the opposite.

## Rejected
- Caps below 128 tokens: no faster, and lower quality.

## Open
- Parallel ranking contexts (office-hours option B) is now the lever. Spreading N=15 over 4 contexts instead of
  2 at cap 128 should bring the p90 below 2s if the contexts really run in parallel on Metal. Unmeasured.
- The branch `feat/rerank-max-doc-tokens` is kept unpushed for that experiment. The plan said to discard it on
  "none". The user decides.
